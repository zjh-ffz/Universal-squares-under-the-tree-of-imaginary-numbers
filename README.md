Hello everyone, welcome to the Chinese/international distribution site for the **Universal Squares under the Tree of Imaginary Numbers** modpack.

## About

**Universal Squares under the Tree of Imaginary Numbers** is a modpack built around the idea of myriad worlds, a blank world waiting to be shaped, and the coexistence of magic and collapse. The world is born for us and shaped by you; once shaped, it holds both magic and collapse. At times it grows with time, and that growth may bring destruction. Everyone develops along a different path, but this world can still be observed and seen.
